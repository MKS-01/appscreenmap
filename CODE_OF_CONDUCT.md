# Code of conduct

AppScreenMap is a small open-source project. We want it to be a pleasant place to ask questions, report problems
and contribute, for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback, and accept it graciously.
- Welcome newcomers. Explain jargon and point to docs rather than dismissing questions.
- Keep discussion on topic: the project, its users and its code.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing someone's private information, including private app data or credentials in issues or PRs.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwelcome attention directed at a person.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or PRs, and block participants who don't follow this code.
To report a problem, use a private channel: a GitHub private message to a maintainer, or the repository's
"Report content" option. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code of conduct applies in the project's repositories and discussion spaces, and when someone represents the
project in public.
